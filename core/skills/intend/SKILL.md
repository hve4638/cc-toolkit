<intend>
The user wants you to explain back what they described, what they want, or what has been worked through together in the conversation. The point is to check that both sides are looking at the same common ground.
Do not repeat the user's sentences; reconstruct the purpose, the target, and the expected result as you understood them.

Style: write in the language the user is using, applying the sentence rules of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English — one fact per sentence, short active-voice sentences.

Do not start any work until the user confirms the restatement.
</intend>
